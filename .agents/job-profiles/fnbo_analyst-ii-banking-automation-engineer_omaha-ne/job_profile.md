## About the Job (as posted)

At FNBO, our employees are the heart of our story—and we’re committed to their success! Please see below the details of this career opportunity and how it fits into our organization’s success.

Summary of the Job:

The Banking Automation Engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining automated solutions that improve efficiency, reduce manual processes, and enhance operational accuracy across key banking functions such as Risk, Operations, Finance, and Treasury.
This role develops automation solutions using Python, RPA tools, and cloud-based technologies, including services within Amazon Web Services (AWS), to support scalable and production-ready workflows. The Banking Automation Engineer partners with Senior Banking Automation Engineers and cross-functional teams to deliver reliable automation solutions that integrate with enterprise data platforms and reporting tools.
The ideal candidate combines technical skills with a process improvement mindset and has a foundational understanding of how to leverage cloud environments to build resilient, maintainable automation solutions within a regulated banking environment.

About This Role:

Job Responsibilities

    Design, develop, and implement automation solutions using Python, RPA tools (e.g., Blue Prism), and workflow platforms
    Build automation scripts to support data processing, reporting workflows, and operational processes
    Translate defined requirements into efficient, scalable, and maintainable automation solutions
    Develop and deploy automation workflows using services within Amazon Web Services (AWS)
    Utilize AWS services such as:
        Compute (e.g., Lambda, EC2) for running automation processes
        Storage (e.g., S3) for handling input/output data
        Scheduling and orchestration (e.g., EventBridge, Step Functions)
    Support secure access and integration with internal systems while adhering to enterprise security and compliance standards
    Identify opportunities to eliminate manual, repetitive tasks across reporting and operational processes
    Partner with Senior Banking Automation Engineers and stakeholders to improve process design prior to automation
    Test automation solutions to ensure accuracy, completeness, and reliability
    Validate outputs against source systems and business expectations
    Support deployment of automation solutions into production environments (including AWS-based deployments) and monitor performance
    Work with structured data using SQL and cloud platforms such as Snowflake
    Document automation logic, workflows, and AWS architecture for maintainability and auditability
    Follow established coding standards, naming conventions, and version control practices
    Ensure compliance with internal controls, audit requirements, and regulatory expectations
    Work closely with Senior Banking Automation Engineers, Data Analysts, and Data Engineers to support end-to-end solutions
    Participate in intake and discovery processes to understand automation requirements
    Provide support and troubleshooting for existing automation processes

The Ideal Candidate for This Role:

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

    Foundational understanding of automation concepts, including scripting, RPA, and workflow automation
    Basic understanding of cloud computing concepts, particularly within Amazon Web Services (AWS)
    Understanding of data structures, relational databases, and data processing concepts
    Proficiency in Python for automation, data processing, and scripting
    Experience or exposure to RPA tools such as Blue Prism
    Basic to intermediate SQL skills for querying and validating data
    Ability to work with AWS services such as:
        Lambda, S3, EC2, or similar cloud services
    Strong problem-solving skills with attention to detail and accuracy
    Ability to debug, troubleshoot, and optimize automation workflows
    Ability to translate requirements into scalable automation solutions (local and cloud-based)
    Ability to follow structured development, testing, and deployment processes
    Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
    Ability to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders
    Strong willingness to learn and adapt to new tools, technologies, and cloud platforms

Required

    Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, or a related field
    1–3+ years of experience in automation, data processing, or software development
    Experience with Python or similar programming languages
    Basic experience with SQL and relational databases
    Foundational experience or exposure to Amazon Web Services (AWS) or similar cloud platforms

Desired

    Experience with RPA tools such as Blue Prism
    Familiarity with cloud data platforms such as Snowflake

    Familiarity with version control systems (e.g., Git)
    Experience working in banking, financial services, or regulated environments
